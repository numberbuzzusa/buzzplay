📖 How to Play

Farm & Dairy Empire is BuzzPlay's featured idle tycoon game. You start with a small plot of land and $50 of in-game money, grow grass, keep goats and cows, and slowly build a dairy business with machines, a cheese room and a flour mill. Everything on this page is 100% free: all money in the game is pretend in-game currency, there are no real-money purchases of any kind, and your farm's progress auto-saves in your own browser every second.

  1. Buy your first Grass Patch. You start with $50. A Grass Patch costs $15 and grows fresh grass every second — grass is the foundation of your whole farm, so this is always the first purchase.
  2. Sell grass or save up. Tap "Sell Grass" in the Market to turn grass into money at $1 each, and save toward your first Goat, which unlocks once you own 2 Grass Patches.
  3. Add goats, then cows. Goats automatically eat grass and produce milk. Own 3 goats to unlock Cows, which produce much more milk. Sell milk at $2 per litre to fund your growth.
  4. Unlock the machines. Milking Machines double all milk production, the Butter Churn turns milk into butter worth $25 each, the Cheese Maker ages milk into cheese worth $60 each, and the Flour Mill adds a steady flour income.
  5. Grow your Empire Value. Your Empire Value — money plus everything you have built and stocked — sets your rank, from Tiny Plot all the way to Dairy Legend. Bigger Land boosts all production by 50% per purchase.
  6. Come back any time. Your farm saves itself automatically in your browser, so your animals, machines and money are exactly where you left them on your next visit.

🏆 Pro Winning Tips

  • Reinvest early, sell late. In the first minutes, almost every dollar should go back into grass and goats. Selling everything for a quick payout slows down the production that makes you rich later.
  • Never sell all your grass. Goats and cows eat grass to make milk. If your grass hits zero, milk stops — always keep a grass buffer growing before you sell.
  • Respect the unlock order. The shop unlocks in a chain — grass → goats → cows → machines → churn → cheese → mill → land. Buying in that order is almost always the fastest path.
  • Processing beats raw milk. A litre of milk sells for $2, but the milk that goes into one butter sells for $25 and into one cheese for $60. Once churns and cheese makers unlock, let them work instead of selling all your milk raw.
  • Watch the 15% price rise. Every purchase of the same item raises its next price by about 15%. Sometimes buying the next unlock is cheaper than buying one more of what you already have.

🧠 Strategy

Think of your farm as a value chain: grass feeds animals, animals make milk, and machines turn milk into goods worth far more than the milk itself. Five litres of milk sold raw earn $10; churned into butter, the same milk earns $25; aged into cheese, about $60. That is why the mid-game is about balance, not just buying more animals — if your churns and cheese makers sit idle because you sold all the milk, your most profitable production is wasted. Keep milk flowing to the machines first, and sell only the surplus.

Empire Value is your true score: it counts your cash, everything you have spent on buildings and animals, and the market value of your stored produce. Because spent money still counts, buying production is never "wasted" — it converts cash into rank. Bigger Land is the late-game king: each purchase multiplies all production by 1.5, so two purchases more than double your whole farm's output. Aim to unlock it once your Empire Value passes $20,000, then watch your grass, milk and flour rates jump together. And remember — production ticks every second while this page is open, so an efficient farm layout earns fastest while you watch it grow.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is the money in Farm & Dairy Empire real money?

No. Every dollar in the game is pretend in-game currency that exists only inside the game. The game is 100% free — there are no real-money purchases, no deposits and no way to withdraw or exchange in-game money for anything.

Where is my farm saved?

Your farm is saved automatically, every second, in your own browser's local storage on your own device (the save is called "buzzplay_farm_v1"). Nothing is uploaded to a server. Your farm will be waiting when you return on the same device and browser — but clearing your browser's site data, or using private browsing, will erase it.

Does my farm keep producing while I am away?

Production ticks every second while this page is open in your browser — including while you scroll and play. When you close the page, your farm is saved exactly as it is and simply waits for you; it does not change while you are gone.

How do I reset my farm and start over?

Tap the "Reset Farm" button at the top of the game and confirm. This permanently deletes your money, animals, machines and Empire Value and starts you again with a small plot and $50, so only do it if you really want a fresh start.

Why do prices keep going up?

Each time you buy an item, its next price rises by about 15% — this is the classic idle-game balance that keeps expansion interesting. Your production grows much faster than prices do once machines and Bigger Land come online.
