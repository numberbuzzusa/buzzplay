About BuzzPlay USA

BuzzPlay USA is a free online browser games portal created by Shahiryar Munir, part of the NumberBuzz USA family of free web projects (alongside NumberBuzz USA and CreatorKit).

Every game here runs entirely in your web browser — no downloads, no installs, no accounts and no sign-ups.
