📖 How to Play

Tic-Tac-Toe is the classic pencil-and-paper game of X's and O's, and on BuzzPlay you can play it three ways: against an easy computer, against an unbeatable computer, or head-to-head with a friend on the same screen. You always play as ❌ and move first. The game is instant — no download, no sign-up — and your win tally is saved automatically in your browser.

  1. Pick your mode. Use the mode switcher above the board: 😊 vs Easy for a relaxed game, 🧠 vs Unbeatable for a real challenge, or 👥 2 Players to share the screen with a friend.
  2. Make your move. Tap or click any empty square to place your ❌. In the computer modes the computer answers with its ⭕ after a short pause; in 2-player mode, players simply take turns tapping.
  3. Complete a line of three. Get three of your marks in a row — across, down, or diagonally — before your opponent does. The winning line is highlighted on the board.
  4. Watch for the draw. If all nine squares fill up with no line of three, the round is a draw and neither side scores.
  5. Start the next round. Tap "New Game" to clear the board and play again. Your ❌ wins, ⭕ wins and draws are counted in the tally under the board and saved for your next visit.

🏆 Pro Winning Tips

  • Take the center first. The center square is part of four different winning lines — more than any other square. If it is free on your first move, grab it.
  • Corners beat edges. A corner square sits on three winning lines, while an edge square sits on only two. After the center, corners are the strongest squares on the board.
  • Block before you build. If your opponent has two marks in a line with the third square empty, block it immediately — even if you were one move away from your own line elsewhere.
  • Think one move ahead. Before every move, ask two questions: "Can I win right now?" and "Can my opponent win on the next move?" Answering both prevents almost every silly loss.
  • Use Easy mode to practise patterns. The easy computer plays randomly, so it is perfect for learning to spot winning lines quickly before you take on the unbeatable mode.

🧠 Strategy

The real secret of Tic-Tac-Toe is the fork: a move that creates two separate lines of three at the same time. Your opponent can only block one of them, so the other completes on your next turn and you win. Watch for positions where one move threatens two lines at once — and just as importantly, watch for your opponent setting up a fork against you. If you see two of their lines building toward the same empty square, that square is the fork point: take it yourself before they can.

Against the Unbeatable computer, perfect defence is the goal. The computer plays flawless Tic-Tac-Toe, which means it can never be beaten — but it can always be held to a draw. Open with the center or a corner, keep blocking every two-in-a-row, and never let it create a fork. If you survive to a full board, you have matched a perfect player, which is the best result anyone can get. In 2-player mode, the same ideas become a mind game: set traps, threaten quietly, and make your friend choose which line to block.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tic-Tac-Toe on BuzzPlay really free?

Yes. The game is 100% free to play in your browser — no download, no account, no sign-up and no purchases. Just open the page and tap a square.

Can anyone beat the Unbeatable computer?

No — and that is by design. The unbeatable mode plays perfect Tic-Tac-Toe, so the best possible result against it is a draw. Beating the Easy computer, or holding Unbeatable to a draw, are the two achievements to aim for.

How does 2-player mode work?

Choose the "2 Players" mode and take turns on the same device. One player is ❌ and the other is ⭕; the status line above the board always shows whose turn it is.

Does the game save my wins?

Yes. Your tally of ❌ wins, ⭕ wins and draws is stored in your own browser's local storage, so it is still there when you come back on the same device and browser.

What is the best first move in Tic-Tac-Toe?

The center square is the strongest opening because it belongs to four winning lines. A corner is the second-best choice. An edge square is the weakest opening and makes forks against you much easier.
