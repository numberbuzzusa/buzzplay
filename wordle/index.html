How to Play Wordle Unlimited

  1. Type any valid five-letter English word as your first guess, using your real keyboard or the on-screen keyboard, then press Enter.
  2. The tiles flip to reveal your clues: green means the letter is in the word and in exactly the right position, yellow means the letter is in the word but in a different position, and gray means the letter is not in the word at all.
  3. The on-screen keyboard keys change colour to match, so you can see at a glance which letters are confirmed, possible or eliminated.
  4. Use the clues to shape your next guess. Green letters should stay locked in place, yellow letters must move to a new position, and gray letters should be avoided.
  5. You have six tries to find the hidden word. Guesses must be real words — if a guess is not in the word list, it will not be accepted and you can try another.
  6. Solve the word to extend your winning streak. When a round ends, tap New Word for the next one — there is no daily limit, and your statistics (played, wins, current streak and best streak) save automatically.

Pro Winning Tips

  • Open with a vowel-rich starter. Words like AUDIO, RAISE, CRANE or SLATE cover the most common letters and reveal the maximum information on guess one.
  • Spend guess two on brand-new letters. If your opener gave you little, do not waste the second guess re-testing the same letters — probe a fresh set of common consonants instead.
  • Respect the yellows. A yellow letter is guaranteed to be in the word, just not where you put it. Every later guess should include it in a different position.
  • Beware of double letters. Words like APPLE, SASSY, LEVEL and EERIE break many solvers. If your clues refuse to fit any normal word, try repeating a letter.
  • Think in letter frequency. E, A, R, O, T, L, I, S and N appear most often in English five-letter words. Rare letters like Q, X and Z are long shots until the clues point at them.
  • Read the pattern, not just the letters. Common endings such as -IGHT, -OUND, -ATCH and -ALLY can unlock a board quickly once two or three letters are known.

Wordle Unlimited Strategy

Strong Wordle play is an information game for the first two guesses and a solving game after that. Your opener and follow-up should, between them, test about ten different high-frequency letters — this almost always reveals at least one green and one or two yellows, which shrinks the possible answers from thousands to a handful. From guess three onward, play "hard mode" even though the game does not force it: reuse every confirmed letter, because guesses that ignore known clues waste your scarcest resource, which is turns. The classic endgame trap is the one-letter-off family — _ATCH could be BATCH, CATCH, HATCH, MATCH, PATCH or WATCH. Guessing them one by one burns a turn each and can easily cost you the round. Instead, spend one guess on a "burner" word that contains several of the candidate letters in other positions (for example, CHAMP tests C, H, M and P at once) and let the colours tell you which family member is the answer. Finally, protect your streak late: on guesses five and six, never submit a word you have not checked letter-by-letter against every clue on the board.

Wordle Unlimited is the classic Wordle word game with the daily limit removed. Every round has a new hidden five-letter word and you get six tries to guess it — and you can play as many rounds in a row as you like.

How many tries do I get in Wordle?

You get six guesses per word. If you have not found the word after six tries, the round ends, the answer is revealed, and your winning streak resets — but you can start a new word immediately.
